Privacy Policy
隐私政策

Last updated / 最后更新：2026-08-30

This Privacy Policy explains how Wintouch Group (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you communicate with us through our business channels, including WhatsApp Business, email, and our internal customer relationship management system.

本隐私政策说明 Wintouch Group（以下称“我们”）在您通过我们的业务渠道（包括 WhatsApp Business、 电子邮件及我们的内部客户关系管理系统）与我们沟通时，如何收集、使用、存储和保护您的个人信息。

1. Information We Collect 我们收集的信息

  • Contact details you provide: name, phone number, email address, company name and role.
  • The content of messages you send us, including WhatsApp messages, emails and attachments.
  • Business transaction records: enquiries, quotations, proforma invoices, orders and shipment information.
  • Basic technical metadata required to deliver messages, such as timestamps and delivery status.

包括：您提供的联系信息（姓名、电话号码、邮箱、公司名称及职务）；您发送给我们的消息内容 （WhatsApp 消息、邮件及附件）；业务往来记录（询盘、报价、形式发票、订单及出运信息）；以及为投递消息所必需的 基础技术元数据（时间戳、送达状态等）。

2. How We Use Your Information 信息的使用方式

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide customer service.
  • To prepare quotations, invoices and order documentation.
  • To keep you informed about the status of your orders and shipments.
  • To maintain internal business records as required for our commercial and legal obligations.

我们仅将信息用于：回应您的咨询并提供客户服务；制作报价、发票及订单文件；向您通报订单与出运进度； 以及为履行商业和法律义务而保存必要的内部业务记录。

3. WhatsApp Business Messaging 关于 WhatsApp 业务消息

We use the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta to exchange messages with customers. Messages you send to our business number are received and stored in our own customer relationship management system so that our team can respond to you. We send messages only to people who have contacted us or have otherwise agreed to receive messages from us, and you may ask us to stop messaging you at any time.

我们使用 Meta 提供的 WhatsApp Business Platform 与客户往来消息。您发送至我们业务号码的消息 将被接收并存储在我们自有的客户关系管理系统中，以便团队回复您。我们仅向主动联系过我们、或已同意接收我们消息的人 发送消息；您可随时要求我们停止向您发送消息。

4. Data Storage and Security 数据存储与安全

Your information is stored on servers controlled by us and is accessible only to authorised employees who need it to perform their work. We apply access controls, encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS) and regular backups to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss or disclosure.

您的信息存储于我们自行控制的服务器上，仅限因工作需要的授权员工访问。我们通过访问权限控制、 传输加密（HTTPS/TLS）及定期备份，防止信息遭到未经授权的访问、丢失或泄露。

5. Sharing of Information 信息的共享

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share information only where necessary to deliver our services — for example with messaging and email service providers, or with logistics providers to arrange delivery of your order — and where required by applicable law.

我们不会出售、出租或交易您的个人信息。仅在为提供服务所必需时共享信息 （例如消息与邮件服务商，或为安排货物运输的物流服务商），以及在适用法律要求时依法披露。

6. Data Retention 信息的保存期限

We retain business correspondence and transaction records for as long as necessary to serve you and to meet our accounting, tax and legal obligations. When information is no longer required, it is deleted or anonymised.

我们在为您提供服务所需、以及履行会计、税务与法律义务所需的期限内保存业务往来与交易记录。 信息不再需要时，将予以删除或匿名化处理。

7. Your Rights 您的权利

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate, request deletion, or ask us to stop contacting you. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable period.

您有权要求查阅我们持有的您的个人信息、更正不准确的信息、要求删除，或要求我们停止与您联系。 如需行使上述权利，请通过下方联系方式与我们联系，我们将在合理期限内回复。
